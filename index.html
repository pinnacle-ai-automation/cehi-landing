What is Beck?

Beck is an intelligent conversational AI assistant designed to automate client booking, scheduling, and communication for service-based businesses. Whether you're in home inspections, consulting, contracting, or any service industry, Beck streamlines your workflow and converts inquiries into confirmed bookings.

Key Features

Intelligent Booking Assistant

Conversational AI that collects client information, schedules appointments, and confirms bookings—all without manual intervention.

Calendar Integration

Seamless Google Calendar integration for real-time availability checking and automated appointment creation.

Automated Communication

Sends booking confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups via email—keeping clients informed every step of the way.

Document Management

Organizes client files, reports, and documentation automatically in Google Drive with a clean folder structure.

Enterprise Security

Bank-level encryption and secure data handling ensures your client information remains protected.

White-Label Ready

Customize Beck for your brand and business needs. Deploy once, scale to any industry.
